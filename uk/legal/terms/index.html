02 / Audit engagements

If you commission an audit

We agree the scope, the price, and the timeline with you in writing before any invoice goes out. These terms apply on top of what we confirm for your engagement, and what we confirm for your engagement wins where the two conflict.

  • 01

    What we deliver

    A single written PDF audit report containing the scored findings, the prioritised action plan, and implementation notes, plus an optional 1h walkthrough call. Exact scope for each audit type is listed on the relevant audit page. The report is sent to you once it is finished and payment has been received; see Fees below.

  • 02

    What we need from you

    Read-only access to your website analytics, Search Console, and (for e-commerce or SaaS audits) any product or content management system. Timely answers to clarifying questions. Decision-maker review at delivery so we can close the engagement cleanly.

  • 03

    Timeline

    Five to eight business days for the SaaS, e-commerce and website audits, and three to five for the technical audit, counted from the moment we have your brief and the access we need. Timelines pause if we are waiting on access or answers.

  • 04

    Revisions

    One round of clarification questions is included. Additional implementation support is available as a separate engagement.

03 / Fees

Fees, payment, and cancellation

Fees are fixed and agreed in writing before you commit. Two rules govern everything below: we never send the report before payment has been received, and once the report has been sent the fee is non-refundable.

  • 01

    How buying an audit works

    You get in touch and we agree the scope and the price. If you decide to buy the audit, we send you an invoice and start work on it. When the audit is finished and your payment has been received, we email you the PDF report. We do not release the report before payment has cleared, and we will not ask you to pay before you have decided to go ahead.

  • 02

    Cancellation by you

    You can cancel at any time before the report has been delivered. Tell us, and we stop working on your audit. If the invoice is still unpaid, nothing is owed and there is nothing to settle. If you have already paid, we return the full amount. Once the report has been delivered, the fee is non-refundable.

  • 03

    Cancellation by us

    We can decline or end an engagement at any time before delivery: if the scope changes drastically, if the work requested would mislead users, or if continuing would create a conflict of interest with another client. If you have already paid at that point, we return the full amount.

  • 04

    Taxes

    Prices are exclusive of VAT or sales tax where applicable. Reverse charge applies for VAT-registered business customers in the European Union.
