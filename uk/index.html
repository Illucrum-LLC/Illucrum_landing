03 / Process

How an Illucrum audit works

Every audit follows the same process, whichever sector you're in. The deliverable is the same too: a scored report with prioritised fixes and a clear action plan.

  1. Intake (Day 1)

    A written exchange or a short call to understand your business, your goals and what success looks like for you.

  2. Crawl and tooling (Days 1 to 3)

    Research into your business and industry, plus automated crawls and tool-based analysis.

  3. Manual review (Days 3 to 5)

    The 52-point checklist applied by hand. This is where the real findings come from; tools alone miss roughly half of what an experienced auditor catches.

  4. Scoring and report (Days 5 to 8)

    Each finding is scored by impact and effort. The result is an audit with a prioritised action plan, not a 200-page data dump.

06 / FAQ

Common questions

The questions that come up before most projects. The full FAQ has its own page.

What's the difference between the three audits?

Different types of business need their websites to do slightly different jobs, so it only makes sense to approach each type of site a little differently, taking its specifics into account. SaaS websites focus more on the customer acquisition funnel, whereas e-commerce sites need clean, useful catalogue and product pages that can be found directly on Google.

How long does an audit take to deliver?

Five to eight days from intake to final report. We don't promise 24-hour manual audits, because there's no such thing. A useful audit takes the time needed to properly understand and analyse your website and your competitors.

Does the audit include implementation?

No. The audit is a standalone deliverable. You can make the changes yourself, or we can help with that as well, though that would be a separate service.

How is this different from running an SEO tool?

A tool gives you data. An audit interprets that data in light of your situation and needs, prioritises what matters and tells you what you can safely ignore.
