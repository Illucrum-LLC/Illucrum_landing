About

A small consultancy, focused on doing one thing properly

Illucrum is an SEO consultancy. Not a full-service agency, not a content marketing shop, not a freelancer with a website. The product is a structured 52-point SEO audit, delivered as a written report with prioritised fixes.

01 / Who runs it

Meet the founder

Illucrum is run by Szymon Kokot, a software developer turned SEO consultant.

SEO audits are a particular kind of work. They call for research into the company, its industry and its website, and for data gathered through both automated tools and manual testing, so that a clear picture emerges of where the problems lie and where the opportunities are. Most software developers know how to build brilliant websites, but that counts for little if no one ever sees them.

Szymon's background spans SaaS, e-commerce and service businesses, with a focus on start-ups and SMEs that have outgrown their own SEO knowledge, from both a development and a business perspective. That understanding of what SMEs need is what the company is built on: getting them the service they need without paying over the odds for things they don't. The main specialism is SaaS audits, with e-commerce as the secondary specialism.

02 / Method

How an audit actually takes shape

Our methodology is at the heart of what we do; you can't really know us without knowing how we work.

  1. We research and adapt

    We don't run the same audit, in the same way, for every client. We get to know each one and adapt the deliverable so that the audit is actually useful.

  2. We gather data with automated tools and by hand

    Some data can, or even must, be gathered with automated tools. But we don't stop there: we also carry out our own manual searches and read through your website, to give you something no automated tool can.

  3. We understand how SEO has evolved

    Years ago, SEO was all about keywords and statistics. These days the lines between marketing, SEO and content creation have blurred. Search engines and AI tools aim to be helpful to people, so optimising purely for a machine is no longer enough.

  4. We prioritise

    Once we have a clear picture, we prioritise the issues to fix and the improvements worth making, so your business gets the most out of its website.

04 / Commitments

What we commit to

The three principles below apply to every audit. They're worth spelling out, in the interest of transparency.

  • 01

    Understanding

    This is what sets us apart most from any automated tool. We don't just hand you the data; that's the easy part. Interpreting it, and drawing conclusions that make a real difference to your business, is the hard bit.

  • 02

    Transparent pricing

    The audit price is on the pricing page. No discovery call just to see a figure. We don't charge each client according to what we think they can afford. No 'starting from' prices hiding upsells. Fixed prices; you choose.

  • 03

    Implementation is your call

    The audit is a standalone deliverable. We won't push you into a retainer; in fact, we don't think SMEs need one. If you'd like help with implementation, we can scope that separately. If you'd rather take the report and act on it yourself, that's exactly what it's written for.

05 / Approach

Why not a retainer

The standard SEO arrangement is a monthly retainer first, an audit somewhere along the way, and action points discovered as you go. That model exists because it's profitable for the agency, not because it's good for the client.

That model may suit large international companies that can spend that kind of money without batting an eyelid. For a start-up or SME, though, committing to a retainer is rather a lot. The good news is that you almost certainly don't need one. There's little point over-optimising something that will only hold for the next couple of months.

An audit is more than enough to tidy up your website, make it clean and indexable, and point you in the direction worth taking. You don't need an audit every month; one or two a year is plenty.

If you're an SME or a start-up, there's no sense burning money on a retainer; a one-off audit is the way to go. The difference in price is huge, whilst the difference in results is modest.
