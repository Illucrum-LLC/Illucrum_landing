Sound familiar?

“My pages aren't on Google”

Only the homepage is indexed, Search Console keeps saying “Crawled, currently not indexed”, or products never appear in results.

The audit finds what's keeping them out of the index.

“Google shows the wrong thing”

Google rewrites your titles and descriptions, or picks a different page than the one you set as canonical.

The audit shows which page Google should see, and why it picks another.

“We never appear in AI answers”

Your buyers ask ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews, and your site is never the one they cite.

The audit checks whether AI assistants can reach, read and quote your pages.

“We get traffic, but no customers”

Visitors arrive from searches that don't match what you sell, or land on pages that don't lead anywhere.

The audit checks whether each page matches what its visitors are looking for.

“We've dropped off the map”

Your map rankings stalled, dropped after a profile edit, or the listing was suspended.

The audit goes through your Google Business Profile point by point.

Questions, answered

Do you implement the fixes?

No. The report is written so you or your developer can act on it, with every fix explained and in order.

How long does it take?

5 to 8 days for the full audits, 3 to 5 days for the technical audit.

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics, and a few lines about your business. Nothing on your site is changed.

When do I pay?

The invoice comes before the audit starts. The report is sent as soon as it's paid.

Which audit do I need?

The one built for your kind of site. If you're not sure, send me your website and I'll tell you which fits.
