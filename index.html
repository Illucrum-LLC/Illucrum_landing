Three questions a GEO audit answers

Most brands have no idea what AI answers say about them. These are the three things you find out, in that order, and each one changes what you should do next.

    Are you in the answer at all?

    We run your category, comparison and problem-led prompts across the major engines and record every mention, every citation, and every competitor that shows up where you do not.

  • 02

    Can the engines even reach you?

    AI vendors run three separate crawler classes, and they do completely different jobs. Blocking the wrong one removes you from answers entirely, and most sites that block AI bots block the wrong one by accident.

03 / Process

How an Illucrum GEO audit works

One process, one deliverable: a scored report with prioritised fixes and a clear action plan.

  1. Intake (Day 1)

    A written exchange to understand your business, your category, and the questions your buyers actually ask before they decide.

  2. Prompt set and crawl (Days 1 to 3)

    We build a prompt set of 10 to 15 queries from your buyers' real language, agree it with you, then run it logged out across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot, plus Google AI Overviews and AI Mode.

  3. Manual review (Days 3 to 5)

    The 52-point checklist applied by hand. No tool reports why an engine skipped you, so this is where the findings come from. Expect 10 to 14 hours of audit work before a word of the report is written.

  4. Scoring and report (Days 5 to 8)

    Each finding is scored by impact and effort. The result is a prioritised action plan, not a folder of screenshots.

06 / FAQ

Common questions

The questions that come up before most engagements. The full FAQ lives on its own page.

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO is about ranking on a results page. GEO, Generative Engine Optimisation, is about being retrieved, extracted, attributed and cited inside the answer an AI model writes. The two overlap, but only a minority of the URLs AI engines cite also sit in Google's top 10 for the same query. A site can rank first and be invisible to ChatGPT; a site can be cited by Perplexity while ranking on page three.

How do you measure something that changes every time you ask?

By running a fixed prompt set of 10 to 15 queries, two to three times each, across several engines, logged out and with personalisation off, then recording the pattern rather than a single output. Individual answers vary. The pattern of which brands and which sources keep appearing is stable enough to act on, and the prompt set becomes your baseline for measuring whether anything changed.

How long does an audit take to deliver?

Five to eight business days from intake to final report. Running the prompt set properly takes time, and so does tracing each gap back to a cause on your site.

Does the audit include implementation?

No, the audit is a standalone deliverable. You can implement the changes yourself, or we can scope that separately as a fixed-price project.
