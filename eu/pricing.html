06 / FAQ

Pricing FAQs

The questions that come up most often about cost, tiers, and payment.

Why is the SaaS and e-commerce audit more expensive than the Website audit?

SaaS and e-commerce audits require more specialised analysis: funnel-stage content checks, faceted navigation review, schema deep-dives, conversion path analysis. The Website audit covers the general SEO fundamentals without those extras. The price reflects the difference in scope, not in quality.

Is there a discount if I book multiple audits at once?

Not as a standard policy. If you operate multiple distinct sites and want all three audited, we can scope a multi-site engagement; the price will be fair but isn't published. Send a short message describing the sites and we'll come back with a number.

Can I upgrade from Basic to Full after I see the report?

Yes. Within 14 days of Basic delivery, you can upgrade to Full by paying the difference. We'll deliver the additional 14 checks and an updated report. Most clients who upgrade do so because the Basic audit surfaced something they want investigated more deeply.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

Not as a standalone product. Implementation support is scoped after an audit, against the specific findings. The reason: monthly retainers without a starting audit produce work that looks productive but doesn't fix the underlying issues. See audit vs retainer for the full explanation.

How do payments work?

Audits are paid in full upfront via bank transfer or Stripe (card or invoice). The work is delivered on the scheduled day or your money back, no questions asked.
