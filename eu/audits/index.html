06 / In scope

What every Illucrum SEO audit includes

Every SEO audit Illucrum delivers includes a 52-point check across technical SEO, on-page elements, keyword strategy, UX, and AI search readiness, with vertical-specific sections layered on top. (The focused technical audit is a separate 36-point engineering review.) You get a written report, a prioritised action plan, every check scored and explained, and a debrief session to walk through findings.

  • Always in scope

    Checks every audit runs

    Technical SEO foundations (page speed, Core Web Vitals, HTTPS, mobile, sitemap, robots, canonicals, structured data).

    On-page SEO (titles, meta descriptions, headings, content quality, internal linking, image optimisation).

    Keyword strategy (current rankings, opportunity mapping, content gap).

    UX and conversion (CTA architecture, navigation, trust signals, engagement signals).

    Vertical-specific section (SaaS funnel, e-commerce catalogue, or the general framework depending on audit).

  • What you receive

    Documents and follow-up

    A written report (PDF) with every check scored, explained, and prioritised.

    An action plan ranked by impact, not a flat list of "fixes".

    A 1h debrief on Microsoft Teams or Google Meet, your call.

07 / Process

How it works

The audit process is the same for every audit we run. You order the audit, send a short brief, and the audit lands in three to eight working days depending on the tier. After the report, a debrief walks through priorities and the most important things to action upon.

  1. Brief us

    Tell us your URL, your business type, and what you want the audit to answer. A WhatsApp message or short email is enough.

  2. We audit

    We start researching, gathering data, and we write the audit report. A quick overview, every section scored, and an actionble implementation plan.

  3. You get the report

    Once the payment is received and the report is finished, it is sent to you, normally within 3 to 8 days. Findings, scores, and a prioritised action plan. No vanity metrics.

  4. We debrief

    A 1h call debrief on Teams or Meet, if you need it. Walk through priorities, decide what to action first, and ask anything the report didn't answer.

08 / FAQ

SEO audit questions, briefly answered

Three questions that come up before nearly every audit. The full FAQ has the rest.

How do I know which SEO audit is right for me?

Pick by site type. If your business runs on a SaaS product with a trial, funnel content, and an integrations directory, the SaaS audit is the right fit. If you run an online store, the e-commerce audit covers catalogue and product schema depth a general audit can't. Everything else fits the Website audit.

What's the difference between the Basic and Full tier?

The Basic tier covers the conversion-critical checks: 35 of the 52 points on the SaaS audit, 34 of 52 on the e-commerce audit, and 27 of 52 on the website audit. The Full tier is the complete 52-point review, adding the backlink profile section, deeper competitor and SERP work, the full AI search readiness layer, and a more comprehensive action plan. Basic is a strong standalone diagnostic; Full is the full picture and roadmap.

How long does the audit take to deliver?

A Basic audit lands in three to five working days from when you send the brief. A Full audit takes five to eight working days because of the deeper backlink, competitor, and vertical-specific work. The focused technical audit lands in three to five working days. The debrief is scheduled within a week of report delivery, at a time that fits you.
