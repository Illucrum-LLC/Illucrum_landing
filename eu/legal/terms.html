02 / Audit engagements

If you commission an audit

A separate statement of work confirms scope, price, and timeline for every engagement. These terms apply on top of any statement of work, and the statement of work wins where they conflict.

  • 01

    What we deliver

    A written audit report, a scoring spreadsheet, a prioritised action plan, and (for Full audits) an optional 30-minute walkthrough call. Exact deliverables for each audit type are listed on the relevant audit page.

  • 02

    What we need from you

    Read-only access to your website analytics, Search Console, and (for e-commerce or SaaS audits) any product or content management system. Timely answers to clarifying questions. Decision-maker review at delivery so we can close the engagement cleanly.

  • 03

    Timeline

    Five to eight business days from kick-off to delivery for most audits. E-commerce Full audits may run up to nine business days. Timelines pause if we are waiting on access or answers.

  • 04

    Revisions

    One round of clarification questions is included. Additional implementation support is available as a separate engagement.
