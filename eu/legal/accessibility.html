01 / Commitment

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to read this site, contact us, and commission an audit.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. The site is built with semantic HTML, uses sufficient colour contrast, supports keyboard navigation, and avoids reliance on motion. We test in screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA) and across major browsers.

02 / Known issues

Known issues

Honest reporting of gaps. If you find more, please tell us.

  • – 01

    Decorative images

    Logos in the header and footer are marked as decorative via empty alt text because the brand name appears next to them. Screen readers will announce the brand name, not the logo.

  • – 02

    External resources

    Linked third-party tools (Google Analytics opt-out, provider help pages) are not under our control. Their accessibility is the responsibility of the providers.

03 / Report a barrier

If something blocks you

The fastest fix is an email; we will reply within five business days.

Write to szymon.kokot@illucrum.com. Please tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and which assistive technology you use (if any). We will confirm receipt, investigate, and let you know what we can fix and when.
