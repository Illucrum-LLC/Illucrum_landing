07 / Process

How it works

The audit process is the same for all three verticals. You order via WhatsApp or email, send a short brief, and the audit lands in three to eight working days depending on the tier. After the report, a debrief walks through priorities and the first five things to action.

  1. Brief us

    Tell us your URL, your business type, and what you want the audit to answer. A WhatsApp message or short email is enough.

  2. We audit

    Our 46-point framework runs across your site. Every check is scored, evidenced, and tied back to a business outcome. Vertical-specific sections layer on top.

  3. You get the report

    A written report (DOCX and PDF) lands in 3 to 8 working days. Findings, scores, screenshots, and a prioritised action plan. No vanity metrics.

  4. We debrief

    A 30 to 45 minute debrief on WhatsApp voice or video. Walk through priorities, decide what to action first, and ask anything the report didn't answer.

08 / FAQ

SEO audit questions, briefly answered

Three questions that come up before nearly every audit. The full FAQ has the rest.

How do I know which SEO audit is right for me?

Pick by site type. If your business runs on a SaaS product with a trial, funnel content, and an integrations directory, the SaaS audit is the right fit. If you run an online store, the e-commerce audit covers catalogue and product schema depth a general audit can't. Everything else fits the Website audit.

What's the difference between the Basic and Full tier?

The Basic tier covers the conversion-critical checks, roughly 26 to 32 of the 46 framework points depending on audit type. The Full tier is the complete 46-point review with deeper backlink analysis, competitor work, and a more comprehensive action plan. Basic is a strong standalone diagnostic; Full is the full picture and roadmap.

How long does the audit take to deliver?

A Basic audit lands in three to five working days from when you send the brief. A Full audit takes five to eight working days because of the deeper backlink, competitor, and vertical-specific work. The debrief is scheduled within a week of report delivery, at a time that fits you.
