01

Search results

What people search for, and what Google does with your titles and descriptions.

  • 44%

    of Google searches include a brand name. The other 56% are generic.

    SparkToro and Datos, 332 million US searches, 2024

  • 33.4%

    of pages in Google's top 10 are shown with a different title from the one in their title tag.

    Ahrefs, 953,276 pages, 2021

  • 50.8%

    of the titles Google replaces are taken from the page's H1 heading.

    Ahrefs, 953,276 pages, 2021

  • 62.8%

    of meta descriptions are rewritten by Google in the search results.

    Ahrefs, 20,000 keywords, 2020

  • +8.5%

    organic traffic after moving the location to the start of the title tag on clinic location pages. The gain came from more clicks, not higher rankings.

    SearchPilot controlled split test, 2025

02

Content and headings

What the data says about length and structure.

  • 96.6%

    of pages get no traffic from Google at all.

    Ahrefs, around 14 billion pages, 2023

  • 1,447

    words is the average length of a top-10 result, with no relationship between length and position on the first page.

    Backlinko, 11.8 million search results

  • +4.5%

    organic traffic after the first H2 on travel listing pages was changed to an H1, in a controlled split test.

    SearchPilot, 2025

  • 53.4%

    of pages cited in Google's AI Overviews are under 1,000 words. Length and citation showed almost no correlation.

    Ahrefs, 560,346 AI Overviews, 2025

03

Speed

Where speed makes its difference: what visitors do after the click.

  • 2.5 s

    or less is Google's threshold for a good Largest Contentful Paint, measured at the 75th percentile of real visits.

    Google, web.dev

  • +21.6%

    more visitors reached the form submission page on lead-generation sites after mobile load times improved by 0.1 seconds.

    Deloitte for Google, 37 brands, 30 million sessions, 2020

  • 3×

    higher conversion rate for B2B sites that load in 1 second than for those that take 5 seconds.

    Portent, 27,000 landing pages, 2022

04

Local search and reviews

What decides the map results, and what reviews do for the business behind them.

  • #1

    ranking factor for the map results is the primary category on your Google Business Profile, according to 47 local search specialists.

    Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors, 2026

  • 47

    Google reviews on average for businesses in the top 3 local results, against 39 for local businesses overall.

    BrightLocal, 93,845 businesses, 2018

  • 270%

    higher purchase likelihood for a product with five reviews than for one with none.

    Spiegel Research Center, Northwestern University, 2017

  • 4.0-4.7

    stars is where purchase likelihood peaks. It falls as ratings approach a perfect 5.0.

    Spiegel Research Center, Northwestern University, 2017

05

AI search

How AI answers are changing what people click.

  • 18%

    of Google searches produced an AI summary in March 2025, roughly one in five.

    Pew Research Center, 68,879 searches by 900 US adults, 2025

  • 8%

    of visits with an AI summary ended in a click on a regular result, against 15% without one. Only 1% clicked a link inside the summary.

    Pew Research Center, 2025

  • 0.61%

    organic click-through rate on queries with an AI Overview, against 1.62% on queries without one.

    Seer Interactive, 25.1 million impressions, 2025

  • +35%

    higher organic click-through rate for brands cited inside the AI Overview than for brands that aren't.

    Seer Interactive, 2025

06

What Google has said

Three common questions, in Google's own words.

  • Word count

    “From our point of view the number of words on a page is not a quality factor, not a ranking factor.” John Mueller, Google

    Search Engine Journal, 2021

  • H1 headings

    “Your site is going to rank perfectly fine with no H1 tags or with five H1 tags.” John Mueller, Google

    Search Engine Journal, 2019

  • Keywords in URLs

    “I believe that is a very small ranking factor. So it is not something I'd really try to force.” John Mueller, Google

    Search Engine Roundtable, 2016
