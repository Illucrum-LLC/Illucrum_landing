01 / Controller

Who we are

The entity that decides why and how personal data is processed on this site.

This site is operated by Illucrum LLC (Limited Liability Company), registered at 5900 Balcones Drive 21473, Austin TX, 78731-4257. For anything related to your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, or complaints, contact szymon.kokot@illucrum.com.

02 / Data

What we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. The categories below are exhaustive.

  • 01

    Contact data

    When you email, message, or call us, we receive your name, email address or phone number, and the content of your message. We keep this for as long as we are working with you and for up to 36 months afterwards for tax and records purposes.

  • 02

    Audit data

    To produce an audit, you grant us access to your website analytics, Search Console, and crawl data. We do not need or want customer-level personally identifiable information from your platform.

  • 03

    Technical data

    Our hosting provider records your IP address, browser, device, and request times in standard server logs for security and abuse prevention. Logs are retained for up to 30 days.

  • 04

    Analytics and advertising data

    If you accept analytics cookies, we collect anonymised usage data through Google Analytics 4. If you accept advertising cookies, we share conversion events with Google Ads and Reddit Ads. Details, including cookie names and providers, are in the cookie policy.

03 / Purposes

Why we use it, and our legal basis

Every category above maps to one specific purpose and one legal basis under data protection law.

Data Purpose Legal basis
Contact data Reply to your enquiry, agree on scope, send proposals and invoices Performance of a contract; legitimate interest in answering enquiries
Audit data Deliver the SEO audit you commissioned Performance of a contract
Technical / server logs Security, abuse prevention, debugging Legitimate interest
Analytics cookies Understand how visitors use the site Consent (you can withdraw it any time)
Advertising cookies Measure and personalise Google Ads and Reddit Ads Consent (you can withdraw it any time)
04 / Processors

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers we need to operate the site and run the business.

  • 01

    Google (Analytics, Tag Manager, Ads)

    Operates analytics and ad measurement under our instructions. Anonymised IPs and Consent Mode v2 signals are used. Data is processed in the European Union and the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses.

  • 02

    Reddit (Pixel / Ads)

    Receives conversion events when you accept advertising cookies. Processing is in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses.

  • 03

    Email and messaging providers

    Used to receive and reply to your messages on email, WhatsApp, and Telegram. Each provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to messages held on their systems.

  • 04

    Accountants and tax authorities

    Invoices and contracts are shared with our accountants and submitted to tax authorities to the extent required by law.

We never sell personal data. We never share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond what you explicitly consent to in the cookie banner.

05 / Your rights

Your rights

You can exercise any of these by emailing szymon.kokot@illucrum.com. We will reply within one calendar month.

  • 01

    Access and portability

    Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, in a portable format.

  • 02

    Correction

    Ask us to fix incorrect or incomplete data.

  • 03

    Deletion

    Ask us to delete data we no longer need. We may keep records we are required to keep by law (invoices, tax records).

  • 04

    Object and restrict

    Object to processing based on legitimate interest, and ask us to restrict processing while we look into it.

  • 05

    Withdraw consent

    Withdraw analytics or advertising consent at any time from the cookie banner; click Manage cookies in the footer to reopen it.

  • 06

    Complain to a regulator

    If you live in the EEA or the UK, you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you may have additional rights, including the right to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; we honour Global Privacy Control signals.

06 / Retention and transfers

How long we keep it, and where it lives

Retention is purpose-bound; international transfers happen only under appropriate safeguards.

  • 01

    Retention

    Contact and audit data: for the duration of our engagement and for up to 36 months afterwards. Invoices and tax records: 7 years (or longer where required by local law). Server logs: 30 days. Cookie consent records: 12 months.

  • 02

    International transfers

    Some of our processors (Google, Reddit, messaging providers) operate outside the EEA and the UK. Transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and, where relevant, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

  • 03

    Security

    We use TLS for all site traffic, strong passwords, two-factor authentication on critical accounts, and least-privilege access on the tools we use. No system is perfect; we will notify affected users without undue delay if a breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights.

07 / Changes

Changes to this policy

If something material changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change, and we will tell active clients by email.

This is version 1.0 of the policy. For questions, write to szymon.kokot@illucrum.com.
