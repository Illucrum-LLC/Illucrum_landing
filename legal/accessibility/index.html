Accessibility statement

The standard we aim for, what we have done, the problems we know about, and how to reach us if something on the site gets in your way.

01

The standard we aim for

We want everyone to be able to read this site, contact us and use the checker. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

02

What we have done

  • Pages are plain HTML with real headings, lists and landmarks, so screen readers and keyboard users can find their way around.
  • Links, buttons, questions and menus work with a keyboard, and the element in focus is clearly outlined.
  • Text colours have strong contrast against their background, and the layout adapts to zoom and small screens.
  • There is very little animation, and it switches off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Every page can be read without JavaScript. Only the contact form and the checker need it.

Problems we know about

  • The Clutch review widget and badge come from another company, and we can't fully control how accessible they are. The same reviews can be read on clutch.co.
  • The sample report PDF is not fully tagged for screen readers. Email us and we will send you the content in an accessible format.
  • The checker's result tables are wide and scroll sideways on small screens. The CSV download contains the same data.
  • The contact form opens in a window built with JavaScript. If it doesn't work for you, email us instead.

How we checked

This statement is based on our own review of the site. It has not had an independent accessibility audit yet. The date at the top of the page shows when we last reviewed it.

05

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site gets in your way, email us with the page, what you were trying to do and, if you like, the assistive technology you use. We reply within five working days and tell you what we can fix and when.

Questions about this page?

Write to us. The person who reads your email is the person responsible for everything described here.
