Cookie policy

Which cookies and similar browser storage this site uses, what each one does, and how to change your mind. The short version: one small record of your choice, and Google Analytics only if you accept it. Nothing for advertising.

Privacy policy

01

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar store that stays in the browser and is not sent with each request. The rules are the same for both: anything that is not strictly needed to run the site needs your consent first. This page covers both.

There are two kinds on this site. Strictly necessary ones make the site work and need no consent. Analytics ones measure how the site is used and only run if you accept them.

02

Everything this site stores in your browser

This is the full list. The names are set by the providers and can change; when they do, we update this table.

Name Set by What it does Type Kept for
ic_consent_v1 Illucrum (local storage) Remembers your cookie choice, so the banner doesn't come back on every page. Strictly necessary 12 months, then we ask again
_ga Google Analytics Tells visits from the same browser apart, so they can be counted. Analytics, only with consent 2 years
_ga_<ID> Google Analytics Keeps track of the current visit. Analytics, only with consent 2 years
__cf_bm Cloudflare Tells people from automated bots to protect the site. Set only when Cloudflare's bot protection is active. Strictly necessary 30 minutes
cf_clearance Cloudflare Records that your browser passed a Cloudflare security check, so you are not asked again. Set only if you were shown one. Strictly necessary 30 minutes by default

Before you choose, and if you decline, Google Analytics sets no cookies and sends nothing.

03

Content loaded from other companies

Some parts of our pages come from other companies' servers. They store nothing in your browser for us, but like any web server they receive your IP address when your browser asks them for something.

  • Google Tag Manager loads the analytics tag and holds it back until you accept. It sets no cookies itself.
  • CDNFonts serves the typeface used on the site.
  • Clutch shows our reviews in a widget and a badge. Clutch may set its own cookies inside them, under Clutch's own cookie policy.

04

Changing your mind

  • Reopen the banner from Manage cookies in the footer of any page, or here. Your new choice applies straight away: Change cookie settings
  • Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes your choice, and the banner appears again on your next visit.
  • Your browser can block or delete cookies for every site. Look for Privacy or Site settings.
  • Google offers a browser add-on that turns Google Analytics off on every site: Google Analytics opt-out

Declining analytics changes nothing about how the site works. Every page, the contact form and the checker work the same either way.

05

What we don't use

No advertising or remarketing cookies, no social media pixels, no fingerprinting and no session recording. If that ever changes, this page and the banner will say so before anything new runs.
