The free bulk checker

The checker is free and needs no account. It fetches up to 25 addresses per run, reads the HTML each server sends back and shows what it finds. It does not run JavaScript, so it shows what a crawler that doesn't render pages would see.

Fair use

  • Check pages you are allowed to access, at a reasonable pace. The limit of 25 pages per run is there on purpose.
  • Don't use the checker to overload or attack a website, or to get around its security.
  • Don't try to reach private networks or internal addresses through it. The relay blocks them.

We may limit or block use that looks automated or abusive, and we may change the checker or stop offering it. It is provided as it is: we don't guarantee that it is always available or that its results are complete and error-free. What you do with the results is up to you.

To use it you need a current browser with JavaScript turned on. Nothing needs to be installed.

This is the whole process, from the first message to the report.

  1. Prices are published on this site and are the same for everyone. The price that applies is the one shown on the day you confirm. See the prices
  2. You get in touch and tell us about your site. We confirm which audit fits and what access we need.
  3. If you decide to go ahead, we send you the invoice.
  4. We run the audit.
  5. You pay the invoice.
  6. We send you the report. That ends the transaction.

What you get

Exactly what is listed on the page of the audit you chose: the point-by-point review for that type of site and a written report with every point scored and prioritised. One round of clarification questions about the report is included.

What you don't get

Implementation, monitoring, retainers or subscriptions. Nothing is sold to you during or after the audit. The report stands on its own: act on it yourself, give it to your developer, or hire anyone you like.

What we need from you

Read-only access to the tools the audit uses, such as Google Search Console or Google Analytics, and answers to our questions. The delivery time published for your audit starts when we have that access and pauses while we are waiting for it.
