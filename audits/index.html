Choosing an audit

Which audit should I pick?

The one built for your kind of site: SaaS for software with a trial or demo, E-commerce for online stores, Local business if most customers find you on the map, and Website for service firms and most other sites. Technical and GEO cover one layer in depth. If you're unsure, ask and I'll tell you which fits.

Do I need more than one audit?

Usually not. Every site-type audit already includes a technical section and an AI search section. The Technical and GEO audits are for when you want that one layer covered in depth.

How long does an audit take?

5 to 8 days for the SaaS, E-commerce, Local business, Website and GEO audits. 3 to 5 days for the Technical audit.

Do you fix what the audit finds?

No. The report explains each fix clearly enough for you or your developer to act on it, in order of priority.
