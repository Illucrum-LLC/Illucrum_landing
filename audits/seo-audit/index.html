01 / Quick answer

What is an SEO audit?

The featured-snippet answer, in plain language.

An SEO audit is a structured diagnosis of why a website is not ranking, converting, or growing the way it should. For small businesses and SMEs, the Illucrum audit reviews 52 points across eight sections: technical SEO, on-page SEO, keyword opportunities, competitor analysis, local SEO, UX and conversion signals, AI search readiness, and the backlink profile. The output is a written report with scored severity and prioritised fixes, delivered in 5 to 8 business days.

04 / Scope

The eight sections of an SEO audit

The 52 audit points are grouped into eight sections. Every check is scored on severity (green / amber / red) and prioritised by likely impact.

  1. Technical SEO (12 points)

    Page speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile friendliness, HTTPS, XML sitemap and robots configuration, canonical handling, structured data, crawl errors and index coverage, redirect chains and 4xx errors, duplicate and thin pages, and hreflang on multilingual sites. Most quiet ranking suppressors hide here.

  2. On-page SEO (10 points)

    Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, content quality and length, and internal linking, plus URL structure, social meta tags, content freshness, and keyword placement. This is also where cannibalisation and zombie pages (indexed, no traffic) surface.

  3. Keyword opportunities (6 points)

    Ranking keyword overview, striking-distance keywords sitting in positions 4 to 20, and local keyword coverage. The audit also covers search intent alignment, the keyword gap against competitors, and the branded versus non-branded traffic split.

  4. Backlink profile (5 points)

    Total backlinks and referring domains, toxic and spammy links, anchor text distribution, links lost and gained over the last 90 days, and the link gap against direct competitors. 66.5% of all web pages have zero backlinks; the audit identifies what is realistic to close in 6 to 12 months.

  5. Competitor analysis (4 points)

    The top 3 organic competitors are identified and their authority scores benchmarked. The audit also covers a top-pages comparison and a content depth analysis, quantifying the gap.

  6. Local SEO (5 points)

    Google Business Profile optimisation, NAP consistency across the web, and review score and recency, plus local citations and local schema. This is the section the SaaS and e-commerce audits drop; if you serve a geographic market, it is the reason to choose this audit over those.

  7. UX and conversion signals (4 points)

    Clear CTAs on key pages and navigation and site structure, plus bounce rate and session duration and intrusive pop-ups and interstitials. SEO traffic only matters if it converts; this section reviews where the conversion gap is.

  8. AI search and answer engine optimisation (6 points)

    Google AI Overview presence, answer engine visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, and AI crawler access and llms.txt. The audit also covers answer-ready content structure, citation-worthy signals and E-E-A-T, and AI-focused structured data.

06 / Process

How an audit takes shape, in five steps

Same shape for every audit. The detail differs by vertical; the deliverable does not.

  1. Intake

    A short brief: the site, the stage, the question you want answered. WhatsApp or email; no calls required.

  2. Crawl and tooling

    The site is crawled and analysed across the standard toolset (Google Search Console, PageSpeed, SEOptimer, structured-data validators).

  3. Manual Research

    We research your business, industry, we read your website, so we are sure we have a clear picture.

  4. Analysis

    Once the data is gathered, it's time to analyze it. The analysis is done manually, no AI.

  5. Report

    A written PDF report with scored findings, prioritised fixes, and an action plan. Delivered to your inbox.

08 / FAQ

SEO audit FAQ

The questions that come up most often before a website audit. The full FAQ has more.

What is an SEO audit and what does it include?

An SEO audit is a structured diagnosis of why your site isn't ranking, converting, or growing the way it should. The Illucrum audit covers 52 points across eight sections: technical SEO, on-page SEO, keyword opportunities, backlink profile, competitor analysis, local SEO, UX and conversion signals, and AI search readiness. The output is a prioritised, scored action plan, not a generic checklist.

How long does an SEO audit take?

Five to eight business days, counted from the moment we have your brief and the access we need. The window reflects the manual review; a real audit cannot be compressed below it without cutting the part that produces the findings.

Can I do my own SEO audit?

For a small site with low competition, yes, especially on the on-page side. For competitive niches or anything beyond the basics, the bottleneck is rarely knowledge; it is interpretation and prioritisation. The full answer lives at SEO audit vs DIY.

Will an SEO tool do the same job?

Tools surface symptoms; an audit interprets them. Also the tool treats each website the same. We adapt to your website type, but also your industry, to make sure we provide valuable information to you. The full comparison lives at SEO audit vs SEO tool.

What happens after the report?

You implement the findings yourself, you can also hire us to do it for you. There is no obligation to continue. The decision logic lives at SEO audit vs SEO retainer.

Is the audit confidential?

Yes. No client work is published without explicit permission, and no findings are shared externally.
