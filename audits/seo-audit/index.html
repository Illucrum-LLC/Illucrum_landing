Worth knowing before you buy

Is this the right audit for my site?

Yes, if you sell expertise: service firms, consultancies, agencies, B2B companies and professional practices. If you sell software, run an online store or win most customers through the map, the SaaS, E-commerce or Local business audit fits better.

My business has an office. Is the local part scored?

Only if you have an office or service area where you want to be found locally. If not, the three local points are marked N/A and the audit covers 51 points instead of 54.

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4, a short description of what you sell and to whom, and the searches you want to be found for. Nothing is changed. Without access, the points that need it are marked N/A; nothing is guessed.

When do I pay?

The invoice comes before the audit starts. The report is sent as soon as it's paid.
