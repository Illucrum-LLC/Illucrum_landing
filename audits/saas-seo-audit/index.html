Worth knowing before you buy

Is this the right audit for my site?

Yes, if you sell software with a trial, demo or freemium signup. If you sell services, the Website SEO audit fits better. If you only need the technical layer checked, for example after a migration, the Technical SEO audit is enough.

Do you look at the product itself?

No. The audit covers the public marketing site, documentation and other public pages. Anything behind the login is only checked to confirm it is kept out of search.

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4, the category name your buyers use, and the searches you want to be found for. Nothing on your site or accounts is changed. Without access, the points that need it are marked N/A; nothing is guessed.

When do I pay?

The invoice comes before the audit starts. The report is sent as soon as it's paid.
