What the audit covers

36 points in 9 sections

Questions

Worth knowing before you buy

Is this the right audit for my site?

Yes, if you know what to say and need to know whether anything technical is in the way: before or after a migration, after an unexplained traffic drop, or when your developer needs a precise list. If you also want keywords, content and competitors reviewed, pick the audit for your type of site.

Is this a security audit?

No. The security points note what is publicly visible, such as the certificate, security headers and exposed files. Nothing is tested for vulnerabilities.

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console, and context on any recent migration, redesign or traffic drop. Server settings are judged from what the site returns, so no server access is needed.

When do I pay, and what happens after?

You get the invoice before the audit starts, and the report once it's paid. After that, nothing: no follow-up pitch and nothing to cancel.
