Worth knowing before you buy

Is this the right audit for my site?

Yes, if your buyers ask AI assistants before they search, or if you rank in Google but rarely appear in AI answers. It works for any type of site. If you want classic rankings reviewed in full, with a shorter AI section, the Website SEO audit fits better.

Can you guarantee AI assistants will recommend us?

No, and no one can. AI answers change between runs and depend partly on sources outside your control. The audit shows what stands in the way and which fixes come first, with every result recorded by platform and date.

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4, plus Bing Webmaster Tools if you use it. Real questions your buyers ask help shape the test set. Server or CDN logs are optional; without them, two points are marked N/A.

When do I pay?

The invoice comes before the audit starts. The report is sent as soon as it's paid.
