Worth knowing before you buy

Is this the right audit for my site?

Yes, if you sell online, on any platform: Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce or a custom build. If most of your sales happen in a physical shop that customers find on the map, the Local business SEO audit fits better.

Do you check every product page?

No. Every page template is reviewed, plus a sample of categories and products chosen to cover the problem cases, such as products with many variants or out of stock. A finding on a template applies to every page built from it.

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4 and, if you use it, Google Merchant Center. Also the platform you run on, any SEO or filter apps, and the categories that matter most. Nothing is changed, and no test purchase is made.

When do I pay?

The invoice comes before the audit starts. The report is sent as soon as it's paid.
