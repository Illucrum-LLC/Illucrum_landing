Questions, answered

Do you implement the fixes?

No. The audit is a standalone deliverable. Act on it yourself, hand it to your developer, or hire anyone you like.

5 to 8 days for the full audits, 3 to 5 days for the technical audit.

Is the price the same for everyone?

Yes. The prices on this site are the prices. No quotes, no add-ons.

When do I pay?

You get the invoice before the audit starts. The report is sent once it is paid.

What happens after I get the report?

Nothing, unless you want it to. There is no follow-up pitch and nothing to cancel.
