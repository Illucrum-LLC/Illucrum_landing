02 / What we believe

Five operating principles.

Most agency websites talk about values nobody disagrees with. These are the trade-offs we actually make — the ones that decide what work we'll take and what we'll politely decline.

01

Diagnosis before prescription.

We don't sell the fix until we've named the problem. If your traffic is flat, the cause might be technical, content-shaped, intent-shaped, or off-site entirely. Skipping the diagnosis is how agencies end up shipping deliverables that don't move rankings. It's the most honest thing we charge for, and the cheapest thing we sell.

02

Plain language, scored evidence.

Every finding gets a 1–10 score and a one-paragraph explanation written for a business owner. You should never have to translate a report before sharing it with a co-founder, a board, or a developer. If we can't explain why a check matters in plain English, the check doesn't make it into the report.

03

Fixed fee. No retainer pressure.

The deliverable is the audit. Implementation is a separate, optional engagement quoted from the action plan. Nothing recurring is hiding in the contract, and there's no auto-renewal. If you never come back, that's fine — the audit was worth what you paid for it on its own.

04

One person on the work.

The person who replies to your first message is the person who runs the crawl, writes the report, and joins the debrief. No pitching team, no implementation team, no offshore content team. The trade-off is volume — we cap at four new audits per week and book out about six weeks ahead.

05

We say no more than we say yes.

If your site is six weeks old, if you've already had an audit you're not ready to act on, or if the work you need is closer to brand strategy than SEO, we'll say so. A wasted audit is bad for everyone — for you because it didn't help, and for us because it's the kind of result that finds its way into reviews.

Section 01

The audit itself

07 questions

An SEO audit is a structured review of every factor that affects how your website performs in search — technical health, on-page content, keyword strategy, backlinks, competitors, and user experience. Most websites have problems hiding in plain sight: a misconfigured redirect, slow images, thin pages competing for the same keyword, or simply no clear strategy. An audit names those problems, scores them, and gives you a prioritised list to fix.

You need one if you've never had your site reviewed by a specialist, if traffic has plateaued or dropped, or if you're about to invest in content or links and want to make sure the foundation is sound first.

Every audit covers six areas across 46 individual checkpoints: technical SEO (crawlability, indexation, speed, mobile, structured data), on-page SEO (titles, meta, headings, internal linking, content quality), keyword strategy (current rankings, gaps, intent mapping), backlink profile (authority, toxicity, opportunity), competitor positioning, and conversion-relevant UX.

Each checkpoint is scored — pass, warning, or fail — and rolled up into category scores so you can see at a glance where your weakest links are. Findings are written in plain language, with a clear "what to do about it" for every fail.

Free tools — Ahrefs Site Audit, Semrush, Screaming Frog, Google's own Lighthouse — are excellent at producing data. They are not good at telling you which of the 800 issues they found actually matter for your business, which to fix first, or which are noise.

An Illucrum audit uses the same kind of data, but adds the human layer: severity weighted by business impact, interpretation by someone who has worked on similar sites, and a written action plan you can hand to a developer or implement yourself.

The whole site. We crawl every indexable URL up to 10,000 pages by default — which covers the vast majority of SME sites. Technical and on-page findings reflect the full crawl, not a single page.

For deeper analysis (content quality, intent matching, conversion paths) we sample representative templates and key pages rather than reviewing every URL by hand. If your site is larger than 10,000 URLs we'll quote a custom scope.

If you sell software with a free trial, paid plans, or self-serve signup, the SaaS audit is built for you. It focuses on trial-page SEO, comparison content, integration pages, and review-site profiles. If you sell physical goods through category and product pages, choose the E-commerce audit — it covers faceted navigation, product schema, and search-driven revenue paths.

For everyone else — agencies, professional services, brochure sites, content businesses — the Website audit is the right call. When in doubt, message us with the URL and we'll tell you which is the best fit. We don't gain anything by selling you the wrong one.

Essential is the diagnostic tier. It covers the 46-point checklist, severity scoring, and a 30-minute debrief. It will tell you what's broken and what to fix first.

Full adds competitor deep-dives (three competitors, gap analysis, link comparison), keyword opportunity mapping, a content audit pass, and a 60-minute debrief — plus a 14-day Slack/email window for follow-up questions. Choose Full if you intend to act on the report immediately, or if you compete in a crowded space where understanding the field matters as much as understanding yourself.

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we'll send a redacted version of a recent audit — same structure, same depth, with the client and findings anonymised. It's the fastest way to understand whether the format and tone fit how you like to work.
