How I work

A few principles behind every report.

  • Built for your kind of site

    A store, a software company and a local clinic have different problems. Each audit has its own checklist, so the points fit what you actually run.

  • Read, not just crawled

    Tools collect the data. Deciding what matters, and what can wait, comes from reading your site and understanding your business.

  • Written to be used

    Plain English, every finding tied to a page and a figure, and the fixes in order of priority. The report is clear on its own.

  • Nothing guessed

    When a point can't be checked, for example without access to your data, it's marked N/A rather than filled in.
