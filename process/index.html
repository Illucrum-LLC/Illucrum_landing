Four steps, start to finish

The same for every audit.

  1. You get in touch

    Send your website and a line or two about the business. I'll confirm which audit fits, or tell you if another one suits you better.

  2. You receive the invoice

    Along with it, I ask for read access to Search Console and Analytics, and a few details about your goals.

  3. I carry out the audit

    Every point on the checklist is checked and scored. It takes 5 to 8 days, or 3 to 5 for the Technical audit.

  4. You receive the report

    As soon as the invoice is paid, the report is sent to you as a PDF. From there, it's yours to use however you like.

What happens during the audit

Tools collect the data. The rest is a careful reading of your site, point by point.

  • Context first

    What you sell, who buys it and which searches matter. A score only means something against that.

  • Your data

    Search Console, Analytics, PageSpeed Insights and a crawl of the site provide the numbers. Access is read-only.

  • A manual review

    Each point on the checklist is checked by hand, against your real pages. Tools show symptoms; reading the site shows the cause.

  • Scoring

    Every point gets a score from 1 to 10 and a status: good, needs attention or critical.

  • Priorities

    Fixes are put in order: anything blocking search engines or AI crawlers first, then critical issues, then quick wins.

  • The write-up

    Plain English, with each finding tied to a page, a figure and a date, so the report also works as a baseline.

Good to know

What do you need from me?

Read access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4, a few lines on what you sell and to whom, and the searches you want to be found for. Some audits need a little more, such as Merchant Center for online stores; each audit page lists it.

Will anything on my site change?

No. Access is read-only, and nothing on your site or accounts is changed. If access isn't possible, the points that need it are marked N/A rather than guessed.

What if you find something urgent?

If something needs attention straight away, such as important pages blocked from Google, I'll tell you as soon as I find it rather than wait for the report.

When do I pay?

The invoice comes before the audit starts. The report is sent as soon as it's paid.

Can one audit cover several websites?

One audit covers one website. Each additional site needs its own audit.

Tell me about your site

Send your website and the audit you have in mind. If you're not sure which one fits, ask.
