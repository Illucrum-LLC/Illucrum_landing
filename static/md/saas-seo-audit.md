# Illucrum SaaS SEO Audit

The Illucrum SaaS SEO Audit is a fixed-price, point-by-point review of a software company's marketing site against 54 points in 10 sections: technical health, on-page SEO, funnel keywords, conversion pages, feature and integration pages, content, competitors and review platforms, backlinks, user experience and AI search visibility. It is built for B2B and B2C software with a trial, demo or freemium model, where buyers research for weeks, compare products side by side and increasingly ask AI assistants which tool to choose.

You receive a written report with every point scored and a prioritized list of fixes. The price is fixed, published and the same for everyone. The report is the end of the transaction: no retainer, no subscription, and nothing sold afterward.

### 1. Technical SEO (10 points)

Technical health, plus the risks specific to software sites: JavaScript frameworks, app subdomains, login areas and documentation.

| # | Point | What it looks at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Page Speed (Mobile & Desktop) | How quickly key pages load on phones and computers, and what slows them down. |
| 2 | Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) | Google's measures of real visitor experience: how fast the main content appears, how quickly the page responds, and whether the layout jumps while it loads. |
| 3 | HTTPS & SSL Certificate | Whether the marketing site, documentation and signup pages are all served securely, with a valid certificate. |
| 4 | Mobile Usability | Whether pages read and work properly on a phone. |
| 5 | JavaScript Rendering & Indexability | Whether search engines and AI crawlers can see your content, or whether it only appears after scripts run. |
| 6 | Robots.txt & XML Sitemap | Whether crawlers can reach your marketing, pricing, feature and documentation pages, and find them listed. |
| 7 | App Subdomain & Login-Wall Handling | Whether the app, login and account areas are kept out of search without hiding anything that should be found. |
| 8 | Canonical Tags & Duplicate URLs | Whether each page has one main address, with duplicates pointing to it. |
| 9 | Structured Data | Whether the product, company and content are described to search engines in valid, accurate markup. |
| 10 | Index Coverage | How many pages Google has indexed, which ones it has left out, and why. |

| 15 | Pricing Page | Whether pricing is published, easy to find, clear, and able to rank for searches about your price. |
| 16 | Feature Pages | Whether each core capability has its own page that can rank for searches about it. |
| 17 | Content Quality & Depth | Whether key pages answer what a buyer arriving from search needs to know. |
| 18 | Internal Linking | Whether the homepage, feature pages, pricing and content link to each other in a way that guides buyers and crawlers. |

## What you get

- **A written report** covering every point, each scored from 1 to 10 and marked Good, Needs attention or Critical, with a plain-English explanation of what was found.
- **A prioritized fix list.** Anything that stops search engines or AI crawlers from reaching or indexing the site comes first, then other critical issues, then quick improvements, then the rest.
- **Findings specific to your site.** Every finding refers to real pages, figures and examples from your site, not generic advice.
- **Dated figures.** Rankings, links, reviews and AI answers are recorded with the date they were seen, so the report also works as a baseline you can compare against yourself.
- **Points that don't apply are marked N/A** and left out of the scoring, with a short note on why.
- **Yours to act on.** The report is written so you can make the fixes yourself, hand it to your developer, or hire anyone you like.

## What Illucrum needs from you

- **Read access to Google Search Console** (a restricted user is enough) **and Google Analytics 4** (viewer access).
- **The category name your buyers use** for the product, and a short description of your ideal customer.
- **The searches you want to be found for,** if you have a list.
- **The competitors you have in mind** (optional). The audit compares you with the products that actually win your searches, and notes both.
- **Nothing is changed** on your site or in your accounts. Access is used only to read.
- **If you'd rather not give access,** the points that depend on it are marked N/A and the report says which. Nothing is guessed.

## What's not included

- **Implementing the fixes.** The audit finds and prioritizes the issues; the work itself is yours to do or assign.
- **Ongoing monitoring, retainers or subscriptions.**
- **Forecasts of traffic, leads or revenue,** and guarantees of rankings.
- **A review of the product itself:** its interface, onboarding or features.
- **Anything sold afterward.** Nothing is pitched during or after the audit.

## Limits, stated up front

- **The public site only.** The marketing site, public documentation and other public pages are audited. Anything behind a login is only checked to confirm it is kept out of search correctly.
- **Backlinks are a snapshot.** The link profile is recorded as it stands on the audit date. The audit doesn't report links gained or lost over time, because no reliable history of them is available.
- **Link and authority figures are estimates.** They come from third-party data, not from Google, and are used to compare your site with competitors on the same day rather than as absolute numbers.
- **Rankings and AI answers vary** with location, device, personalization and date. The report records what was seen, when and where.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the Illucrum SaaS SEO Audit look at the product itself?

No. It audits the public marketing site, documentation and other public pages. The app behind the login is only checked to confirm it is correctly kept out of search.

### Does the SaaS SEO Audit cover documentation and help centers?

Yes, where they are public and affect search: whether they can be crawled, whether they are served securely, and whether AI crawlers can reach them.

### Why does an SEO audit for software look at review platforms?

Because buyers use them to build a shortlist, they often outrank vendors for "best" and "alternatives" searches, and AI assistants draw on them when they recommend tools. A product with thin or neglected profiles loses ground in all three places.

### Does Illucrum fix the issues the SaaS SEO Audit finds?

No. The audit is the deliverable. The report is written so you can act on it yourself, hand it to your developer, or hire anyone you like.

### Is the price the same for everyone?

Yes. Each Illucrum audit has one fixed price, published on illucrum.com. There are no tiers and no custom quotes.

### When do I pay?

You receive an invoice when you decide to go ahead. The audit is carried out, and the report is sent once the invoice is paid.

### What happens after I receive the report?

Nothing else. There is no follow-up sale, subscription or retainer. The report is yours to use as you see fit.

### Is the report automated?

No. Every point is checked and written up by Szymon Kokot, and every finding refers to your site specifically.

### Can the SaaS SEO Audit guarantee better rankings?

No. Rankings depend on Google and on your competitors, and no one can guarantee them. The audit shows what is holding the site back and which fixes to make first; results depend on what is done with it.
