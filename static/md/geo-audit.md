# Illucrum GEO Audit

The Illucrum GEO Audit is a fixed-price, point-by-point review of whether AI systems can reach, read, trust and cite a business. GEO stands for generative engine optimization: visibility in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. The audit covers 51 points in 7 sections: AI crawler access, the SEO foundations that AI visibility depends on, how content is structured into citable passages, entity and authority signals, structured data, direct testing of what AI assistants say, and how AI visibility can be measured.

You receive a written report with every point scored and a prioritized list of fixes. The price is fixed, published and the same for everyone. The report is the end of the transaction: no retainer, no subscription, and nothing sold afterward.

## Limits, stated up front

- **AI answers change.** Assistants give different answers between runs and over time. The report is a dated snapshot: what was seen, on which platform, on which date.
- **Diagnosis, not citations.** The audit finds what stands between the business and AI visibility, and ranks the fixes. It can't promise that any assistant will cite or recommend the business.
- **Some fixes happen off your site.** Where the gap is presence on review platforms, communities or publications, the report says so. That work is slower than changes to your own pages.
- **Measurement is partial.** Google's AI reporting shows impressions only, analytics misses AI visits that arrive without a referrer, and nothing captures citations that never produce a click.
- **Crawler activity needs logs.** Without server or CDN logs, crawler activity can only be inferred, and those points are marked N/A.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a GEO audit?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. A GEO audit checks whether AI systems such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews can reach a website, read it, trust it and cite it. The Illucrum GEO Audit covers 51 points and ends with a prioritized fix list.

### How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO asks whether a page can rank in search results. GEO asks whether a page can be retrieved, quoted and cited in an AI answer. The two overlap only partly: a page can rank well in Google and never be cited by an assistant. AI systems also depend on their own crawlers, compete at the level of individual passages rather than whole pages, and rely heavily on what other sources say about a business.

### Which AI platforms does the GEO Audit test?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, plus Claude and Copilot where your audience uses them. Every result is recorded with the platform and date.

### Do I need an llms.txt file?

Its effect on AI visibility is unproven. The audit notes whether you have one, but never scores a site down for not having it.

### Can the GEO Audit guarantee that AI assistants will recommend my business?

No. No one can promise that. The audit shows what stands between the business and AI visibility, and which fixes to make first. What assistants say depends partly on sources outside your control.

### Does Illucrum fix the issues the GEO Audit finds?

No. The audit is the deliverable. The report is written so you can act on it yourself, hand it to your developer, or hire anyone you like.

### Is the price the same for everyone?

Yes. Each Illucrum audit has one fixed price, published on illucrum.com. There are no tiers and no custom quotes.

### When do I pay?

You receive an invoice when you decide to go ahead. The audit is carried out, and the report is sent once the invoice is paid.

### What happens after I receive the report?

Nothing else. There is no follow-up sale, subscription or retainer. The report is yours to use as you see fit.

### Is the report automated?

No. Every point is checked and written up by Szymon Kokot, and every finding refers to your site specifically.
