02 / The audit

GEO audit pricing

There is no Basic tier, because the sections gate each other and a partial diagnosis is not worth buying.

03 / Scope

What the price doesn't cover

Stated up front, so nobody discovers it at delivery.

  • 01

    Implementation

    The audit diagnoses and ranks. Putting the fixes live is a separate fixed-price project if you want our help with it.

  • 02

    Content production

    The report tells you which pages need restructuring and why. It does not write them.

  • 03

    Ongoing monitoring

    You get the prompt set to re-run whenever you like. We don't sell a monthly subscription to watch it for you.

  • 04

    Outreach and placements

    The audit identifies which third-party sources the engines draw from in your category. Getting you into them is separate work.

  • 05

    Guaranteed citations

    There are no positions in an AI answer to buy or rank for. What you get is a diagnosis, a tiered plan, and a baseline to measure against.

04 / FAQ

Pricing FAQs

The questions that come up most often about cost, scope, and payment.

Why is there only one tier?

Because the seven sections gate each other. A visibility gap measured in the prompt testing means nothing until you know whether the cause is crawler access, page structure, entity signals, or third-party footprint, and you cannot find that out by checking half of them. A cut-down GEO audit would tell you that you have a problem without telling you what it is.

What is the founding-client rate?

A reduced price for the first ten clients, in exchange for a review on Clutch and permission to write up the engagement as a case study. It is the ordinary trade for being early: you get the work below list, we get the credibility. Once the ten are gone the list price applies.

Is there a discount for multiple sites?

Not as a standard policy. If you operate several distinct sites and want all of them audited, we can scope a multi-site engagement and the price is then negotiable. One domain with several country subfolders or subdomains still counts as one audit.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

No. We don't sell monthly retainers, and we think they make even less sense in GEO than in SEO, because the levers move on a quarterly timescale rather than a weekly one. If you want help implementing the audit findings, we scope that as a separate fixed-price project.

How do payments work?

We agree the scope with you first. If you decide to go ahead, we send an invoice payable by bank transfer and start work straight away. Once the report is finished and payment has been received, we email you the PDF. We keep you posted on progress throughout. You can cancel before the report is delivered and get your money back; after delivery the fee is non-refundable.

Can we re-audit later at a reduced price?

Yes. A repeat audit on the same domain within 12 months is priced lower, because the prompt set already exists and the baseline is already recorded. Ask when you book the second one.
