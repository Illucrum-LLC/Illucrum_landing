00 / Quick answer

How much does an SEO audit cost?

The featured-snippet answer, in plain language.

An Illucrum SEO audit costs between $197 and $597 depending on the audit type and tier. The focused Technical SEO audit is a single fixed price of $197. The Website SEO audit starts at $297 (Basic) and runs up to $597 (Full). The SaaS and E-commerce SEO audits start at $297 (Basic) and run up to $597 (Full). All pricing is fixed, paid before the report is delivered, with no hidden fees.

06 / FAQ

Pricing FAQs

The questions that come up most often about cost, tiers, and payment.

Is there a discount if I book multiple audits at once?

Not as a standard policy. If you operate multiple distinct sites and want all of them audited, we can scope a multi-site engagement. The price then could be negotiable.

Can I upgrade from Basic to Full after I see the report?

Yes. Within 14 days of Basic delivery, you can upgrade to Full. Just contact us and we will get working on it, to deliver a the Full audit report.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

Not really. We don't believe in retainers, not for most of our clients. But we are always happy to adapt, contact us and we will see how we can help you. See audit vs retainer for the full explanation.

How do payments work?

We agree the scope with you first. If you decide to go ahead, we send you an invoice payable by bank transfer and start work straight away. Once the report is finished and your payment has been received, we email you the PDF. We keep you posted on progress throughout. You can cancel before the report is delivered and you get your money back; after delivery the fee is non-refundable.
