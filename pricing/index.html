About the price

Is the price the same for everyone?

Yes. The prices on this page are the prices. No quotes, no tiers, no add-ons.

Does the price depend on the size of my site?

No. Large sites and catalogs are reviewed by page template and sample, so a finding on one template covers every page built from it. The report states exactly what was covered.

When do I pay?

You get the invoice before the audit starts. The audit is carried out, and the report is sent once the invoice is paid.

Is there anything else to pay for?

No. The price covers the whole audit and the report. Nothing is offered or sold afterward, and there is nothing to cancel.
